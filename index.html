About

Design with
real play.

I'm Suhee, a game UX / UI designer. Most of the work starts in playtests, not in Figma — mapping intent, then deciding if the answer belongs in the world, in chrome, or nowhere.

More about me →

Studios hire me for HUD language, menu architecture, and the first hour in-world. The three studies here are stand-ins until you drop in shipped, NDA-safe frames.

What I do

What I actually do.

Combat chrome that scales with actual danger. Persistent vitals stay small; fatal timers get type, not another icon family.
Inventory, crafting, loadouts — readable on pad and handheld. Empty cells stay empty.
Onboarding with no slideshow. The room teaches the verb; the UI only confirms it.
UMG / Unity UI budgets, localization overflow, colorblind combat language, and input that works on mouse, pad, and touch.

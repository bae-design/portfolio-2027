About the practice

I design the layer between player intent and game systems — HUDs, menus, and the first hour — so information earns its pixels.

Most of my work starts in playtests, not in Figma. I map what a player is trying to do, then decide whether the answer belongs in the world, in chrome, or nowhere. The best interface in a game is often the one you stop noticing.

I collaborate with engineering and narrative on implementation constraints early: UMG / Unity UI budgets, localization overflow, colorblind combat language, and input that has to work on mouse, pad, and touch.

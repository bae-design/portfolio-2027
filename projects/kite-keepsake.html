02 — Cozy adventure / hybrid

Kite &
Keepsake

Role

UI / menu architecture

Year

2025

With

Design & narrative

Output

Inventory kit, recipes

A satchel should feel like a kitchen drawer: you know where the good knife lives without counting slots.

Problem

Early grids read as an MMO leftover: grey cells, rarity borders, tooltips that blocked the recipe. Handheld testers could not tell a keepsake from a material at a glance.

Approach

Items are silhouettes first, stats second. Crafting is a short sentence pinned to the bottom edge, so the grid never competes with the action. Empty cells stay empty — no ghost icons, no noise.

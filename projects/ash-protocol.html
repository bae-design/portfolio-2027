Ash Protocol

Role

UX / HUD design

Year

2026

With

Systems & combat

Output

Language spec, HUD kit

If everything on screen is urgent, nothing is. Threat had to become a typographic hierarchy, not a stack of icons.

Problem

Playtests showed players dying to information they had already seen: a red skull, a pulse, a compass pip — all equal. The HUD was shouting in one register.

We needed a language that scaled with actual danger: environmental risk, enemy awareness, and extraction windows could not share the same visual weight.

Approach

Three tiers. Persistent vitals stay small and analog. Spatial threat lives on the compass and minimap. Fatal timers interrupt with type, never with a new icon family.

Color is reserved for the one status that can end the run. Everything else is paper-on-dark, so a colorblind pass does not flatten the hierarchy.
